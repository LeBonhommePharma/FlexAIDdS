What is an Entropy Audit?

The proposed entropy.help workflow would independently recompute finite-sample ensemble summaries from deposited docking outputs and compare them with the originating engine.

The planned checks include:

  • Energy provenance — whether inputs are physical energies or CF/contact-function scoring proxies
  • Finite-sample ledger — log Z, mean score/energy, and Shannon terms under the explicitly declared weighting convention
  • Reproducibility evidence — source revision, binary/input digests, command, seed, and deposited ensemble

A result will not be called physical binding free energy merely because a Boltzmann formula was applied. FlexAIDdS GA scores are CF/contact-function proxies unless an explicit, validated energy calibration and reference-state treatment says otherwise.

Current status: no completed entropy.help audit artifact package is present in this repository. The material below is planning documentation, not validation evidence.

Why provenance matters

Docking scores can omit configurational, vibrational, solvent, concentration, and reference-state contributions. They also may use arbitrary score units. A partition sum over those scores can be useful for checking software consistency, but it does not by itself recover a physical binding free energy.

The planned audit layer is therefore narrower and falsifiable: it will state exactly which ensemble was used, preserve its sampling and multiplicity policy, recompute the declared quantities, and publish the artifacts needed to reproduce that check.

Experimental-affinity or true-ΔG claims remain out of scope unless their additional physical assumptions and validation evidence are deposited explicitly.
