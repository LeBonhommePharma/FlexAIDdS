Operating Principles

The Physics First

01 / THERMODYNAMICS

ΔG = ΔH − TΔS

Every binding event is a thermodynamic equation. Ignoring the entropy term isn't a shortcut — it's a wrong answer. We compute the whole thing.

02 / OPEN SCIENCE

Apache 2.0, Full Stop

Proprietary docking tools gatekeep science behind paywalls. Drug discovery belongs to everyone. Our code ships under Apache 2.0, reproducible by construction.

03 / HARM REDUCTION

Education Over Prohibition

The war on drugs failed the pharmacology exam. Psychoactives are tools with mechanisms — understandable, predictable, teachable. We teach them.

04 / MECHANISTIC RIGOR

No Black Boxes

ML correlation without mechanism is astrology with better marketing. We use neural networks as a last resort, not a first instinct. First principles, every time.
