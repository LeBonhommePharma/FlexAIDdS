## 5. Findings

### F1. Clean sync-merge; no resolution risk (INFO — positive)

- Evidence: §3.1–3.5; only three site files; `merge-tree` identity.
- Why it matters: Pre-landing merges are a common place for silent “accept ours” losses of master security/build fixes. Here master contributed **only** cosmetic stats, and feature science content is fully retained.
- Fix recommendation: None. Pattern is correct for bot-updated site files.

### F2. Master was not a science peer at merge time (INFO)

- Evidence: `git rev-list --count 465c7a611..8772039e7` = **1**; sole commit is `Update: site stats (auto)` by `github-actions[bot]`.
- Why it matters: Operators might assume “merged origin/master” pulled CI, formula, or Metal fixes. It did **not**. Feature still needed independent review of its own 15 commits (PoseBust, DirectLigandIC, oracle ceiling, etc.) — those are out of scope for *this* merge’s delta but in scope for PR #256 as a whole (see sibling audits / `7e79352e3`).
- Fix recommendation: In PR descriptions, phrase as “synced site-stats tip of master” when the unique master range is bot-only.

### F3. Pre-existing `latest-release` mismatch across site pages (INFO — not introduced)

- Evidence: At merge tree, `site/FlexAIDdS/index.html` has `v2.0.2` while `site/index.html` has `v2.0.0`. Present on both parents; merge only advanced commit count/date.
- Why it matters: Marketing/docs inconsistency only; no docking impact. Risk of human misreading “current release” from the wrong page.
- Fix recommendation: Out of band — make `scripts/update_site_stats.py` (or release workflow) keep both markers in lockstep with the true latest tag.

### F4. Site stats are lagging / non-scientific provenance (INFO)

- Evidence: Commit count 1744 and date 2026-07-14 come from cron auto-updater, not from hashing the feature tip or PR #256 payload.
- Why it matters: After landing a large feature, GH Pages stats may still show pre-land counts until the next bot run. Do not treat site `stat-commits` as audit evidence of science SHA.
- Fix recommendation: None for this merge. Prefer `git describe` / binary SHA pins for claim provenance (AGENTS.md).

### F5. No tests executed by this commit (INFO — expected)

- Evidence: Merge delta has zero test or source changes; no CI job is implied by the commit object itself.
- Why it matters: Correct — nothing to re-test *for the merge resolution*. The feature stack still required (and later PR-land path should have) PoseBust gtest + parity suite validation; that is a property of the **branch**, not of this three-way resolution.
- Fix recommendation: Do not mark “merge green” as substitute for `ctest` on PoseBust targets when evaluating PR #256.

### F6. Security / hygiene of merge delta (PASS)

- Evidence: Diff is HTML/JSON stats only; no network hooks, no absolute `/Users/…` paths, no secrets, no license-impacting third-party code.
- Fix recommendation: N/A.

### F7. Parent order enables correct first-parent history on the *feature* branch (PASS)
